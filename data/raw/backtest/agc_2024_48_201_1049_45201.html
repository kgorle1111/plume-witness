Air Monitoring Comparison Values (AMCV) are used to evaluate the potential for effects to occur as a result of exposure to concentrations of constituents in the air. AMCVs are based on data concerning health effects, odor, and vegetation effects. They are not ambient air standards. If predicted or measured airborne levels of a constituent do not exceed the comparison value, adverse health or welfare effects would not be expected to result. If ambient levels of constituents in air exceed the comparison values, it does not necessarily indicate a problem, but rather, triggers a more in-depth review. If you have any questions about the potential for health, odor, or vegetation effects from exposure to the reported concentrations please contact the Toxicology Section by telephone at (512) 239-1795 or by email at tox@tceq.texas.gov.
