Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
A loss of power caused by weather conditions resulted in a dual incinerator trip that caused high pressure in the vent system and an OHC upset. The vents flowed to the EVS where they were scrubbed and emitted to atmosphere. The OHC upset resulted in an expansion joint leak to the atmosphere.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Operations personnel secured the vent and OHC systems to stop the emissions
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
Engineering calculation estimates based on system pressure, temperature, and chemical composition.
