Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
On 1/16/2024, abnormally cold weather conditions associated with Winter Storm Heather resulted in a process upset. Out of an abundance of caution, an initial STEERS report was submitted on that same day for this incident. However, upon further review, it was determined that no reportable quantities were exceeded. Although we are submitting updated emission estimates in this final STEERS report, we are requesting that the STEERS report be retracted, or at a minimum, flagged as a non-reportable event.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Refinery personnel followed established procedures to stabilize the refinery and minimize emissions.

Monitoring data and engineering calculations were used to determine the quantities associated with this event.
