Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
On January 16, 2024, at approximately 06:10, a flaring event occurred as a result of severe weather conditions causing an upset in the process gas compression system. A product compressor valve position feedback device was exposed to moisture, wind, and severely low temperatures causing the device to freeze. This led to a product compressor recycle valve malfunction causing a forward flow increase that was relieved to the flare control device, 1-105A.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Immediate action taken to shut down the plant, relieve excess pressure resulting from the forward flow increase in the compression system, and minimize emissions.The facility added freeze protection to the valve position feedback device to reduce the risk of upset from extreme freeze conditions.
