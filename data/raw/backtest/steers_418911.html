Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
The Houston Plant experienced a multiple boiler trip on 3/15/2024 beginning at approximately 4:30 am. During the startup process post the boilers tripping, a process upset occurred in the VAU which caused flaring. The flaring event resulted in the RQ for butadiene to be exceeded. The root cause investigation is on-going at this time.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Operations took immediate action to stabilized the plant after the process upset. At 21:56 the flaring ceased.
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
The plant flare analyzer and plant flare flow meter were used as the basis for the emissions estimates.
