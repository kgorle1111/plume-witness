Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
A seal failure in one of the benzene pumps caused and ESD shutdown which put the plant in a safe state. This relieved material from the styrene unit to the flare. Emissions at the flare increased. Emissions event is still ongoing at this time.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Production of styrene was shut down to put the plant in a safe operating state. Immediately proceeded with startup operating procedures to return to routine permitted emission rates.
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
Use of online flow, pressure, temperature, concentration analyzers, environmental emission software, and process knowledge to determine material sent to the flare.
