Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
A scheduled external inspection of the internal floating roof (IFR) revealed a hole in the IFR and product on top of the IFR.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Upon discovery, internal notifications were made and response actions initiated. The tank was placed on vapor control at approximately 1200, on June 6, 2024, and remained on vapor control until degassing was completed during the tank cleaning process. Product was transferred from Tank 100-8 to a barge from June 6, 2024 at 1900, to June 7, 2024, at 0305. The remaining product was then transferred from Tank 100-8 to Tank 100-9 starting at 0820 on June 7, 2024 and completed at 1845 same day. Area and fence-line monitoring was conducted, and no readings were detected. Tank cleaning was initiated on June 9, 2024, and completed on June 13, 2024. ITC provided updates to TCEQ, as requested, during the initial stages of the event until the tank was emptied of product. The internal floating roof will be repaired or replaced and inspected prior to returning the tank to product service.
