Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
The feed from the furnace FOL-710 was routed to the decoke pot (EPN V31142) due to an error which in turn vented to the atmosphere. In addition, a 2-inch hole was discovered on the decoke pot header.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Upon discovery of the situation, the operations immediately cut the feed to the decoke pot and re-routed it to the fractionator. The 2-inch hole on the decoke pot header was clamped to stop the leak.
