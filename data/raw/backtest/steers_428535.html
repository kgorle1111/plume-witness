Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
Arkema Clear Lake planned to startup Incinerator #2 on Monday 8/26/24 at 21:00, but start up was delayed until Tuesday, 8/27/2024 at 23:00. Scheduled routine maintenance on Incinerator #2 was completed, and the source returned to normal operation. During maintenance on Incinerator #2 the refractory was cleaned using a series of sonic wave technology, which created fine particulate dust. Several vacuum trucks were used to remove the dust from the incinerator. Although Arkema was concerned that there would be an opacity event when the air blower started up, the opacity monitor on the incinerator stack demonstrated that there was no exceedance of a permit limitation on opacity, nor was there an excess opacity event.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Dust generated from the maintenance activity was removed via vacuum trucks, to the extent practicable. The air blower was started before any process vents or fuel gas was introduced to the incinerator. No exceedance of the opacity limit nor any excess opacity was detected by the opacity monitor on the incinerator stack.
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
Facility air pollution control equipment is operated consistent with good practices for minimizing opacity. Emissions monitoring systems were operational, and detected no exceedance of the opacity limit nor any excess opacity was detected by the opacity monitor on the incinerator stack. The facility has documented the start up event in contemporaneous operating logs and monitoring records.
