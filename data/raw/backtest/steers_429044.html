Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
Propylene Refrigeration Compressor C-600 (PRC C-600) for Ethylene Unit 1594 unexpectedly shut down due to a mechanical malfunction resulting in process gas being routed to the flare. The sudden and unexpected change in fuel gas composition caused furnaces H-104 and H-109 to exceed the hourly routine NOx lb/hr limit.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Feed to the furnaces was reduced, and the PRC was restarted. Unit restored normal operations and minimized flaring.

Emissions were calculated using online monitoring data and engineering estimates.
