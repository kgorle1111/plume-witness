Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
On October 13, 2024, at approximately 4:45 AM, simultaneous equipment trips in the Catofin Area caused a plant shutdown, resulting in a flaring event. It was found that the associated DCS cabinet for the tripped equipment had a temporary power failure on the primary and secondary power supplies.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
The compromised battery in the DCS cabinet was removed. The incoming power supply terminals and wires were cleaned, and a cover was placed over the terminals until a new DCS cabinet is installed. Plant feed (propane, propylene, and fuel gas) were routed to the flare control device to relieve system pressure prior to restarting the plant.
