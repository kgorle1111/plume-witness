Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
On October 27, 2024, at approximately 1:35 PM, the process gas compressor(101J) tripped, resulting in a flaring event. The controller to the deethanizer stripper (107E) feed valve closed, stagnating forward flow of the C3 liquid pretreater (115E) tower bottoms. When the tower bottoms hit a high sump level of 85% it caused the 101J compressor to trip. The cause of the event is still under investigation.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Site personnel cut plant feed (propane, propylene, and fuel gas), and shutdown equipment, as needed, to minimize flaring. Once the tower bottoms reached an operable level, the 101J compressor was restarted.
