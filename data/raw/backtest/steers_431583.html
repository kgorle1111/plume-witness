Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
A high level of seal oil in the overhead seal oil tank caused both the Ethylene Refrigeration Compressor (ERC) and Propylene Refrigeration Compressor (PRC) to shutdown unexpectedly, which resulted in process gas being routed to flares CB701 and CB710.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Operations followed operation procedures to reduce the seal oil tank level to a lower level and restarted both the ERC and PRC, which resulted in flaring being minimized.
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
Emissions were calculated using online monitoring data and engineering estimates.
