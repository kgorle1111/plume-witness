Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
The scheduled startup (STEERS no. 432227) of ethylene unit EU-1594 following a planned outage of EU-1594 is being replaced with an emissions event due to steam supply challenges experienced during the startup. In addition, a sudden and unexpected change in fuel gas composition caused Furnaces H101, H102, H103, H104, H105, H106 and H107 and Boiler PK830 to exceed the hourly routine NOx lb/hr permit limits.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Unit startup was completed using established procedures to minimize emissions.

Emissions were calculated using online monitoring data and engineering estimates. The scheduled startup began on 11/24/2024 11:00 am. On 11/25/2024 11:00 am, discovered the need to replace the scheduled startup with this emission event.
