Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
Emergency Hand switch on the dimerization ethylene feed line was inadvertently pressed causing a control valve to close. The closed control valve resulted in an increase in line pressure which (by design) activated a Pressure Safety Valve vent to the flare.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Pasadena Performance Products utilized good engineering practices and plans during this event. The flare was maintained and operated to ensure vented hydrocarbons were properly combusted.
