Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:

This 13.2kV Bus-C Switchgear, the upstream 138/13.2kV Transformer-C, and associated components are the dedicated electrical systems for the EBSM site's power distribution system (excluding the BRM's which are supplied through temporary 12.47kV commercial pole line).
