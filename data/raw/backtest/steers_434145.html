Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
A high liquid level in the suction drum caused the Cracked Gas Compressor (CGC) to shut down unexpectedly, which resulted in process gas being routed to flares CB701 and CB710.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Operations personnel followed operation procedures to reduce the liquid level in the suction drum and restarted the CGC. Feed to the furnaces was reduced, which resulted in flaring being minimized.
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
Emissions were calculated using online monitoring data and engineering estimates. Discovery of the RQ exceedance was 4:45 am on 1/5/2025. The flaring event began at 3:45 am on 1/5/2025.
