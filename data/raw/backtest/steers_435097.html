Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
This incident is still under investigation and the root cause will not be fully determined until the investigation is complete. On 1/25/2025 at approximately 3:14, while adjusting D-2410 column pressure, a chain valve operator malfunctioned, which caused the pressure in D-2410 to increase, activating a relief valve which routes to the BP2 Emergency Flare (E-B1501B).
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Operations responded to the event by reinstalling the chain valve operator and opening the valve, re-establishing reflux on D-2410. This allowed the pressure on D-2410 to lower below the relief valve setpoint and the flaring subsequently ceased.

Process knowledge and engineering calculations have been used to estimate the emissions.
