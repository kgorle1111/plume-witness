Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
The access ladder on Tank 150-42 came off of the guide rail and penetrated the external floating roof, creating a 2" hole. Stored product (gasoline blendstock) released onto the top of the floating roof. Approximately 9 bbls of gasoline blendstock leaked onto the ground from the roof drain.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Tank movement was shut down immediately. Air quality monitoring was implemented. The tank was emptied to prevent the floating roof from sinking. Vacuum trucks were mobilized immediately to clean the product on the roof and on the ground. The cleanup process took approximately 12 hours and 50 minutes. The tank was removed from service and degassed for repair.
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
The total amount of product lost to the air was calculated by subtracting the amount of product recovered by vacuum trucks from the volume lost in the tank product inventory.
