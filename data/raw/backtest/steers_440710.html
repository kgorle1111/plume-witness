Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
This incident is still under investigation and the root cause will not be fully determined until the investigation is complete. On 05/15/2025 at approximately 13:00, Bayport Plant 3 experienced a process upset when a pump tripped causing the loss of coolant to the D-3310 Epoxidizer, which resulted in a high temperature interlock that caused relief valves to lift and vent to the BPIII Emergency Flare (EPN: E-B1501C) and a relief valve on F-3260 Oxidate Tank to lift to atmosphere.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Operations responded to the event by safely stabilizing the pressure in the process, thereby allowing the relief valves to reseat and the flaring subsequently ended.
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
Process knowledge and engineering calculations have been used to estimate the emissions.
