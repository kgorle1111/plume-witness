Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
The facility experienced a PSV-0585 that lifted to flare and a release from a 3/4" nipple small bore piping that broke at the thread underneath the bleed valve which is in isobutane service.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
The pipeline and PSV were isolated. The facility pipeline was isolated by closing off valves and depressured, which stopped the release. Flaring occurred for restarting of facility equipment for return to normal operations. The Morgan's Point flare was maintained and operated to ensure vented hydrocarbons were properly combusted.

The emissions in this report were based on measured data, engineering calculations, and TCEQ approved flare methodologies.
