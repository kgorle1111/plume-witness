Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
A partial power outage was experienced, and total steam was lost from both the boiler and third-party primary steam provider. This led to a process upset and the 32 Scrub Tower pump tripped, causing pressure to increase in the vent system. The 32 Saponification (Sap) Belt Pressure Safety Valve (PSV) and 32 Turbo Dryer flame arrestor opened to atmosphere intermittently for a 7-minute period. The total emissions from this release are approximately 405.3404 lbs of methyl acetate and 939.2871 lbs of methanol, with methyl acetate exceeding the state default RQ of 100 lbs.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
The 32 Scrub Tower pump was re-started and stopped the releases to atmosphere from the 32 Sap Belt PSV and 32 Turbo Dryer flame arrestor. The entire facility quickly shut down due to lack of steam for a prolonged period of time.
