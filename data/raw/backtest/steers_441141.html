Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
During unit startup from 5/26/2025 @4:52PM to 5/27/2025 @2:20AM, the Methyl Iodide (MeI) concentration in the waste gas to the flare header was high and exceeded the range of the analyzer. Subsequent modeling indicated that the total amount of MeI combusted during the event resulted in an exceedance of the MAERT limits of both Hydrogen Iodide (HI) and Iodine (I2) by a quantity in excess of the state RQ.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
The unit continued startup procedures to reduce the amount of MeI sent to the flare.

PI data, aspen simulations, and engineering calculation were used to estimate quantity released.
