Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
At approximately 1:23 pm on 8/31/25 during a severe thunderstorm, a brief power outage occurred throughout the entire facility complex and caused a process upset, resulting in the Saponification (Sap) production lines to shut down. Sekisui's boiler, the primary vent gas control device, and backup flare both shutdown from the loss of power, leading to a release of vent gas directly to atmosphere from the backup flare stack. Instability on the natural gas header also occurred as a result of the power outage and caused short, intermittent releases of natural gas from the relief valve. Later in the day, Operations was in the process of restarting the 31 Sap line. However, at 23:35, the vent system experienced pressure instability issues and led to a 20-second release to atmosphere of the 31 Turbo Dryer flame arrestor. The initial calculated emissions from this incident estimated that the state default RQ of 100 lbs was exceeded for methyl acetate, but after reviewing process data and finalizing emission calculations, the methyl acetate emissions did not exceed the state default RQ of 100 lbs. Sekisui calculated total emissions to be 349.9367 lbs methanol, 86.4356 lbs methyl acetate, 165.9151 lbs vinyl acetate, 62.9833 lbs acetaldehyde, and 2708.259 lbs of natural gas.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Operation immediately began to stabilize process equipment. At 15:14 the natural gas header pressure was stabilized and the relief valve was no longer intermittently releasing to atmosphere. At 15:39, Operations was able to successfully restart the backup flare and vent gas was no longer releasing directly to atmosphere. When pressure issues occurred during the start-up of the 31 Sap line, vent flow to the 31 Scrub Tower was increased to stop the 31 Turbo Dryer flame arrestor from opening to atmosphere.
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
Process knowledge and data, technical calculations, and operator witness accounts. Initial calculations had estimated that methyl acetate exceeded the state default RQ of 100 lbs, however, after reviewing process data and finalizing emission calculations, the methyl acetate emissions were calculated to be 86.4356 lbs and did not exceed the state default RQ of 100 lbs.
