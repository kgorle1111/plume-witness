Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
This incident is still under investigation, and the root cause will not be fully determined until the investigation is complete. On 9/4/2025 at approximately 08:20, Bayport Plant 2 experienced a process unit upset during start-up of column D-2410. An overpressure in column D-2410 resulted in relief valves (RV), RV-4101A/B, lifting and venting to the emergency flare, E-B1501B. After the overpressure event, RV-4101A/B were discovered to be leaking.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Operations responded to the overpressure event by safely depressuring and shutting down column D-2410, which subsequently ceased the flaring event. Relief valves, RV-4101A/B, were isolated from the process.

Process knowledge and engineering calculations have been used to estimate the emissions.
