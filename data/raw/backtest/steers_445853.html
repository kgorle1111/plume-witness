Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
Pump seal on P-90A failed early in the morning on 9/7/25, resulting in product being sent to wastewater vessel V-16 with emissions to the flare. Around 17:00 operators smelled hydrocarbons in the area around V-16 and roped it off. On 9/8/25 and 06:30 it was found that a nozzle on V-16 was not sealed to the vessel and was leaking vapor to the atmosphere. An initial estimate of air emissions was completed at 13:00 on 9/8/25. Pump P-90A was taken out of service shortly after.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
An attempt was made to start backup pump P-90B at 17:30 on 9/7/25 and it was found to be locked out. Operations on P-90A continued due to emissions that would result from shutting the pump, and therefore, the plant down, and not realizing the amount and composition of vapor leaking from V-16. On 9/8/25 it was determined that there was an electrical issue with P-90B and after several attempts the pump was started at 13:45 on 9/8.
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
1) The composition of the product stream at P-90A, the temperature, and the partial pressure of the components, and 2) an estimate of the air flow from the separation between the nozzle and V-16 vessel.
