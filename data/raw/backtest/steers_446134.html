Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
At 21:53 on September 11, a power interruption caused the unplanned shutdown of Trains 1, 5, 6, 7, and 9. These shutdowns led to excess flaring that surpassed the hourly permit limits for both the North and South flares. During the excess flaring at Monument 2, it was identified that the North Flare pilots were not lit. The pilots were subsequently relit at 01:22.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Operations personnel worked to minimize the quantity and duration of the flaring as much as possible. The venting that was necessary was directed to the North Flare (ES-205, ES-215) and the South Flare System (ES-805, ES-815).
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
The quantity of hydrocarbons sent to the flares was obtained from the North Flare Online Monitoring System and the South Flare Online Monitoring System. NOx and CO emissions were calculated using measured heat load and TCEQ approved emission factors.
