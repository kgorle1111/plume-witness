Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
Plant wide power outage from CenterPoint power grid during severe thunderstorm event. The Monomer column reflux pump went down from the power outage which caused an increase in 1,3-Butadiene vapor in the column. The volume of Butadiene vapor going to the Dry Monomer Surge Drum (V-7212) increased which led to high pressure in V-7212. The increased butadiene vapor and pressure vented to the LP Flare. The boilers were also down from the power outage and the lack of steam contributed to the Visible Emissions.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
CenterPoint restored power at approximately 14:46. Operations restarted the boilers and provided steam to the flare and resumed as a control device for the LP flare header recovery system to minimize the volume of VOC's sent to the flare. Operations was able to restart and stabilize the Monomer column.
