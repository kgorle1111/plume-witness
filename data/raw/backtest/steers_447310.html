Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
On October 7, 2025, at approximately 11:00 AM, a planned maintenance activity inadvertently caused a loss of power to certain control systems. This unanticipated power disruption triggered the activation of the flare system to safely manage excess process materials and minimize potential emissions. As a result, visible flaring occurred at emission points 30FL1 and 30FL6, leading to a reportable quantity (RQ) exceedance. Sulfur dioxide (SO₂) emissions were determined to have been less than the RQ. Other reported EPN contaminants did not exceed any RQs, but are included, as is customary in these final reports.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Refinery personnel promptly followed established emergency procedures to safely stabilize affected units and mitigate emissions. Air monitoring conducted in the surrounding area confirmed no off-site impacts.
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
Continuous Emissions Monitoring Systems (CEMS), Analyzer data and engineering calculations
