Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
Slug Discharge of pollutant(s) into wastewater influent triggered lower explosive level (LEL) safety interlocks. This caused the 1st Step Treatment Tanks to vent an estimated 162.1 lbs. of 1-octene (CAS # 111-66-0) air emissions per calculations completed 10/28/2025, when all sample results were received. This loading exceeded the 100 lbs. reportable quantity (RQ) - there is no specific RQ for 1-octene for federal regulations like CERCLA.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Operations sought source(s) of elevated LEL in influent industrial user waste streams but a source was not identified. After LEL reached safe levels, operations attempted to route process back to RTO 2601. A mechanical malfunction occurred, so Operations began heating RTO 2602. Operations routed 1st step tanks' off-gas to RTO 2602 once chamber temperature was above the permit-required minimum limit. Additionally, GCA continues to look for sources of the 1-octene during the incident.
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
Analytical results for influent wastewater, collected at beginning of the emissions event, were utilized to estimate air emissions. A slug discharge of 1-octene from industrial user(s) is the suspected root cause of this RQ incident. A source has not been identified. If more information becomes available where an industrial user source is identified, GCA will take enforcement action via its TCEQ-approved pretreatment program.
