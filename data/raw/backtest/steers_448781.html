Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
Celanese reported an emissions event starting on 11/10/2025 at 13:23 out of an abundance of caution. Maintenance was being completed on the sensor system in the Makeup Gas (MUG) Compressor. While inspecting a malfunction in one of 3 sensors used to detect abnormal conditions, a second sensor tripped causing the MUG Compressor to shutdown. The shutdown caused vapors within the back half of the unit to be routed to the flare. The unit followed startup procedures beginning at 13:30 and the unit began producing methanol again at approximately 20:10. A comparison of the emissions and the permit MAERT lb/hr emission limits (EPN MEOHFLR) showed that lb/hr emissions were maintained within permit MAERT limits. While following startup procedures, it was later found that two PVRVs opened to atmosphere. Those emissions are below RQ thresholds and are included in this report. These are also reported as FIN/EPN MEOHFUG and are considered a recordable emissions event.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
The MUG Compressor was quickly inspected and determined safe to operate. The MUG Compressor was cleared to be placed back into service. The emissions to flare while restarting the MUG Compressor were determined to be less than shutting down the unit due to the known cause of the trip due to the maintenance. All emissions were routed to the flare (EPN MEOHFLR). A PVRV release was unknown at the time of occurrence. Upon review of process data the next day, an overpressure situation was identified. The unit will review startup procedures to reduce the risk of this taking place again.
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
Flare flow meters, flare emission factors, and process knowledge were used to calculate emissions as required by the permit. Emissions were determined to be below permit MAERT lb/hr emission limits (EPN MEOHFLR). As a result, this event does not exceed any reportable quantity threshold. Please note, the emission limits provided in the air contaminant compound list is for total VOC.
