Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
On November 21, 2025, Nouryon discovered a nitrogen regulator had failed which caused the atmospheric tanks T-7 and T-8 to intermittently vent to atmosphere via the conservation vent as designed resulting in the tanks exceeding the pound per hour MAERT limits. Upon further investigation, it appears T-7 emissions exceeded the 24 hour RQ on 8/16/2025 14:00 to 8/18/2025 20:00 and 8/26/2025 9:00 to 8/27/2025 2:00. T-8 intermittently vented and exceeded the RQ from 5/16/2025 10:00 to 5/19/2025 3:00 and 5/19/2025 22:00 to 5/27/2025 11:00.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
The investigation into the event is ongoing and Nouryon has repaired the nitrogen regulator.
