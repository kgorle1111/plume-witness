Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
During a tank-to-tank transfer operation between tanks 2501 and 2503, an incident occurred that affected Tank 2501 and 2502. Following this event, a structural component associated with the catwalk between Tanks 2501 and 2502 became displaced and contacted a valve associated with Tank 2502. The valve was compromised, resulting in the release of material from Tank 2502. The estimated quantity released is approximately 20,000 barrels of spent sulfuric acid. An investigation is ongoing to determine the cause and contributing factors.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Emergency response procedures were immediately initiated upon discovery of the release. Emergency response contractors were mobilized, and the affected area was secured. Appropriate authorities were notified, including port authorities. Port operations were temporarily suspended to ensure safety and facilitate response and mitigation activities. Response, containment, and assessment actions remain ongoing. NRC was notified at 2:26a Report #145361.
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
The estimated quantity released was based on the known working capacity of Tank 2502 and tank level information available prior to the event. The tank is designed to hold approximately 25,000 barrels, and available information indicates the tank contents were released. The estimate will be refined if additional verified information becomes available as the investigation and assessment continue.
