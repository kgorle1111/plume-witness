Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
The cause of the RQ was an RV leaking by to the flare. The Styrene and EB units were placed on standby for the duration of the freeze event. After the freeze event, activities were performed to stabilize equipment after the freezing weather impact to the units. Upon restarting the units, the EB reactor section experienced frozen impulse lines and unusual excessive structural vibration may have impacted RV-4126 and RV-4127. RVs 4126 and 4127 operate in parallel. RV-4126 was pulled for repairs and left RV-4127 in service with minor leak-by to flare. RV-4126 returned, and RV-4127 was sent out for repairs. The RVs can only be repaired offsite. They must be rebuilt and the work cannot be performed by Styrolution personnel.The emission event or upset began on 1/28/2024 8:00. The emission event that became a reportable quantity above the permit limit occurred at 15:00 on 1/29/2024. The initial report showed a time of 1/29/2024 at 15:00 for this reason.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
The repair for RV-4126 was expedited since it had to be sent offsite to be rebuilt. Reactor pressure during the event never exceeded 75% of RV set pressure during the event to aid in seating of the RV. Once RV-4126 arrived onsite, it was promptly installed and emissions at the flare returned to below permitted limits.
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
Use of online flow, pressure, temperature, concentration analyzers, environmental emission software, and process knowledge to determine material leaking by. Shutting down would result in higher emissions from the flare than the leak-by occurring from RV-4127. The RV was routed to the flare and the flare was operating as intended.
