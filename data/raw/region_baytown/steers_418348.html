Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
Cause of Emission Event: At approximately 7: 20 PM CST on Saturday, March 2, 2024, the site experienced a leak of Carbon Disulfide (CS2) from the bottom of the CS2 Product Tower in the 180 Unit, when a chemical hose failed during a transfer from the tower to a collection tank. The leak immediately caught fire due to the very low flash point of CS2. The fire oxidized the CS2 and generated emissions of SO2. Additional trace amounts of H2S were released during the spill.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
The onsite operations team immediately responded to quickly contain the fire and stop the leak by isolating the valve. The fire was fully extinguished, using water, at approximately 7:55 PM CST. Amid the site response, the plant was safely shutdown. There was no harm to any of our employees or off-site injuries. The plant is implementing repairs including replacing of valves; flushing and cleaning of equipment; and replacing hoses.
