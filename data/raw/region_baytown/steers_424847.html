Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
A leak was discovered in a section of underground product transfer piping located on facility property.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
ITC initially reported this event on May 17, 2024, at approximately 11:00 a.m. to TCEQ via telephone in accordance with the requirements of 30 TAC 327.3, and TCEQ Case # 20241737 was assigned. ITC also notified Harris County Pollution Control via telephone at approximately 11:11 a.m. 30 TAC §101.201(a)(4) provides that "The owner or operator of a regulated entity experiencing a reportable emissions event that also requires an initial notification under §327.3 of this title (relating to Notification Requirements) may satisfy the initial 24-hour notification requirements of this section by complying with the requirements under §327.3 of this title." Because it is not possible to only submit a final STEERS report, ITC is submitting this "initial" notification and a final STEERS notification immediately thereafter on the same day. ITC initiated response actions upon discovery of the release. A vacuum truck was used to remove leaking product and the leak area was excavated to expose the underground piping. The leaking section of piping was identified and then cleared of all product, stopping the leak. Excavation using both standard excavation methods and hydro-excavation continued to further expose the piping in the immediate area to allow for further inspection, evaluation and possible repair. During the excavation process, residual product contained in the surrounding soil was removed using a vacuum truck. The piping section involved will be further evaluated and either repaired or permanently removed from service.
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
Air emissions estimates are based on personnel observations, release characteristics and loading and evaporative loss calculations.
