Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
On 9/27/2024 at approximately 14:00, a small leak on the glycol concentrator valve bonnet developed into a larger leak. The incident was reported to the TCEQ as a reportable emissions event out of an abundance of caution based on initial emission estimates. After further review of the emission event data, it has been determined that this incident did not result in a reportable emissions event for diethylene glycol. Therefore, this STEERS notification is being retracted.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
A clamp was installed over the leaking valve bonnet to stop the emissions event at approximately 21:10 on 9/27/2024.
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
The initial emissions were estimated based on process data and engineering estimates. The incident was reported to the TCEQ as a reportable emissions event out of an abundance of caution based on initial emission estimates and other information regarding the event. However, it has been determined that this event did not result in a reportable quantity of diethylene glycol being released to the atmosphere. Therefore, this STEERS notification is being retracted.
