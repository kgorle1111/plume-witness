Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
Due to the sudden loss of propylene feedstock, the C-Line Production Unit initiated a controlled shutdown. As part of the safety and emission reduction protocols, some unreacted propylene was routed to the flare system, resulting in increased flaring activity. The feedstock interruption was caused by the unexpected failure of the safety manager associated with the propylene supply system.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
To minimize emissions, the plant followed established procedures to isolate and recover all vent streams connected to the flare header that could be safely managed. For vent streams that could not be isolated or recovered, flaring was employed as the most effective mitigation strategy, offering a 99% destruction efficiency.
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
Emissions calculations were based on key operating parameters, including pressure, temperature, feed rate, and unit inventory.
