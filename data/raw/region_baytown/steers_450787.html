Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
To comply with the HRVOC cooling tower regulations, bag samples were caught and analyzed. The results for the bag sample caught on 11/21/2025 were reported to TPC by the external lab on 12/17 at 17:08. On 12/18, emissions were calculated which indicated a potential permit limit exceedance and reportable quantity event. Process technicians worked diligently to determine a potential source for the emissions and found a leaking tube in an overhead distillation column condenser leaking propylene into cooling tower water.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Process technicians began looking for a potential leak into the cooling tower water. Equipment was strategically isolated to find a leaking source. Upon finding a leaking condenser, personnel isolated the condenser from service. The plant was then safely shut down to repair the leaking tube on the identified condenser.
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
Concentration results from bag samples and cooling tower circulation rates were used to calculate emissions. Harris County Pollution Department was also notified.
