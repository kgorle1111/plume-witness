Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
At the Saturated Gas Recovery Unit (SGRU-1242), liquid flooding in the upper section of the Debutanizer (T-2500) tripped the Debutanizer Feed Pumps (P-2490 A/B), which caused light material to be sent to the Rerun Tower (T-2700). This increased the pressure in T-2700 and led to flaring emissions from Flare 19, Flare 22, and Flare 103B. The unit upset also caused hydrocarbon intrusion into the acid gas header, resulting in exceedances at the Sulfur Recovery Unit (SRU-543).
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Operations made process adjustments to stabilize the process unit and stop the emissions.
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
Flow meters, analyzers, and CEMS.
