Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
At an electrical supply substation, a power disruption caused a temporary loss of power at the Valero Port Arthur Refinery and surrounding third-party facilities. The power disruption caused boilers to trip at third-party steam suppliers as well as Valero, resulting in a sudden loss of steam supply to the Valero Refinery. The sudden loss of electrical power and steam supply caused upsets at multiple process units, leading to flaring, along with sulfur plant and heater exceedances.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Operations immediately started following emergency procedures, making adjustments to reduce charge rates on some process units and shut down others to reduce and stop the emissions.
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
Flow meters, analyzers, and CEMS.
