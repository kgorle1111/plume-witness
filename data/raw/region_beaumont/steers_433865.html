Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
At the Crude Distillation Unit (AVU-147), a crude oil preheat exchanger began to leak, resulting in the release of crude oil to the concrete unit foundation. Less than 5 barrels of oil was released to soil. This resulted in VOC emissions to the atmosphere. A portion of this crude oil ignited, causing combustion emissions to the atmosphere.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Response efforts were initiated immediately to stop the leak, extinguish the burning crude oil, and safely posture the unit in order to mitigate the incident and minimize emissions.

Engineering estimates.
