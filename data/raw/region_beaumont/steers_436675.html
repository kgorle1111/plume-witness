Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
The emissions event occurred on the morning of 2/19/2025 due to a failure to initiate the scrubber system prior to running a reaction in Reactor 14. The emission event ended when the reaction operation was completed, an estimated two hours and eighteen minutes after the start of the reaction. The event was not discovered until 02/21/2025, at which time it was reported promptly. Though the scrubber was not running during the reaction, the emissions did pass through the scrubber / emissions control device, SCRUB1EMUL (Emulsions Scrubber). The ensuing investigation found the primary cause to be that the operator controlling the process failed to start the scrubber prior to the start of the reaction. The Standard Operating Procedure for the activity specified that the scrubber should be running during the operation.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Upon discovery of the emissions event, operation of the associated equipment was stopped. Reporting to STEERS was performed. Reporting requirements per CERCLA and EPCRA regulations were followed. An investigation was performed. The Veolia WTS discipline policy has been enforced to correct the failure to follow the standard operating procedure. The incident has been shared widely across the facility to emphasize the importance of ensuring emissions control devices are operated according to procedure.
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
When determining the amount of the suspected release involving a chemical reaction, there are various competing reactions (some of which are harmless). The worst case scenario was assumed when calculating the Estimated Quantity released in this report. The Estimated Quantity released is based on analysis of the chemical reaction, material quantities, assays, equipment specifications, and duration of operation.
