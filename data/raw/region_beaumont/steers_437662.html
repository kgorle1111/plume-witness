Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
On March 15, 2025, at approximately 10:24 hours, the Motiva Port Arthur Manufacturing Complex (PAMC) experienced a process unit upset at its Motiva Refinery (RN100209451; TCEQ Account JE-0095-D) which resulted in flaring from the Lube Hydrocracker Unit (LHCU) Flare Stack. In its Initial
State of Texas Environmental Electronic Reporting System (STEERS) Report, Motiva estimated that a reportable quantity (RQ) for sulfur dioxide might have been exceeded based on initial calculations. However, after further evaluation and final calculations, this flaring event was determined to be below the RQ limit for sulfur dioxide. Therefore, Motiva requests that the Initial STEERS Report be
rescinded. This facility is authorized under New Source Review Permit No. 6056 and 8404.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Operations took immediate actions to stabilize the process unit, reduce emissions, and stop the flaring event. Process streams were routed to the LHCU flare for destruction.
