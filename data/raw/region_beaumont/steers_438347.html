Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
On 3/26/2025 at approximately 17:00 a cargo tank (disconnected from a truck) that was located at the facility's 10 day transfer yard began releasing vapor from its PRV. The facility's emergency response team was activated and the site emergency coordinator activated emergency response procedures. The local fire department was dispatched to the facility. The national response center was notified (Report # 1426949), and the TCEQ Spill Hotline was notified (20251022). Downwind air monitoring with a 5-Gas meter (CO, O2, LEL, VOC, H2S) did not detect any readings during the response phase. The fire department departed the facility at approximately 22:00 as the situation was under control, and there were no longer any visible emissions. The contents of the cargo tank were safely fed to the facility's hazardous waste incinerator on 4/01/2025.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
A coordinated effort between the fire department and site emergency personnel included monitoring temperature and spraying water in order to cool the cargo tank.
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
Emissions were estimated from the hazardous waste manifest and waste information profile associated with this material. Boiling points of constituents was also taken into account when quantifying estimated emissions.
