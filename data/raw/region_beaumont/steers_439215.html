Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
On April 12,2025, at approximately 19:28 hours, the Port Arthur Manufacturing Complex (PAMC) experienced a process upset at its Motiva Refinery (RN100209451; TCEQ Account JE-0095-D) due to the loss of a boiler feedwater pump at the Fluid Catalytic Cracking Unit (FCCU), resulting in process streams being routed to the FCCU bypass stack and flaring at the Alky and FCCU flare stacks.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Operations took immediate actions to stabilize the unit and to stop the release.
