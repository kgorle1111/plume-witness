Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
A drop in the Refinery's 600 psig Steam Header Pressure during heavy rainfall and the loss of supplemental 600 psig steam from 3rd party steam supplier lead to flaring and the loss of NOx reducing steam at the Cogen Unit.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Operations implemented the Emergency Steam Shedding Guidelines to reduce the amount and duration of flaring.
