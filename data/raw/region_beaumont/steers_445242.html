Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
A malfunction occurred in the facility's boiler system, resulting in a loss of steam production. As a result, the site was unable to supply sufficient steam to the steam-assisted flare. The lack of steam support resulted in elevated opacity levels from the flare, exceeding the 5-minute limit. The Flare BTU remained in compliance, and the flare stayed lit throughout the event, with the flame maintaining a steady orange hue. The issue was promptly investigated, and immediate corrective actions helped eliminate excess opacity.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Upon identifying the boiler malfunction, immediate troubleshooting efforts were made to restore boiler operation; however, these attempts were unsuccessful. To mitigate excess opacity, plant personnel reduced the load on the flare, and production rates on associated process columns were reduced. When the steam supply could not be restored promptly, plant personnel immediately worked on safely shutting down two columns, which stopped the excess opacity. Personnel then worked continuously to repair the boiler, and steam service was successfully restored once the necessary repairs were completed.
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
Process knowledge. The boiler malfunction was sudden and outside of Monument Chemical Port Arthur's control, marking the first incident in the last three years. As the plant personnel took immediate action to minimize opacity and to stop excess opacity quickly by shutting down two columns, there was no adverse impact on the environment or human health.
