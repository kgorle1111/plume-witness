Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
The Valero Port Arthur Refinery experienced a total loss of nitrogen from a third-party supplier, which shut down during a heavy rain event. The sudden loss of nitrogen caused multiple process unit upsets, resulting in reportable flaring.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Operations followed established procedures to shut down process units and put others on circulation or trim charge rates to minimize emissions.
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
Flow meters, analyzers, CEMS, and engineering estimates.
