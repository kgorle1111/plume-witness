Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
At the Delayed Coking Unit (DCU-843), the Quench Water Valve (FV-2011A) malfunctioned, sticking at 100% open prematurely. This caused a sudden influx of quench water into the Coke Drum (D-3100), which flash boiled and caused a rapid pressure increase in D-3100 and the downstream Water Seal Drum (D-4020). This pressure increase led to a blow-through of D-4020 to the flare header and reportable flaring at Flare 23 and Flare 26.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Operations immediately blocked in FV-2011A to stabilize the blowdown system pressure and stop the flaring.
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
CEMS, analyzers, flow meters, and engineering estimates.
