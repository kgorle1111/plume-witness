Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
A steam driven compressor experienced inlet steam instability resulting in an unexpected process compressor shutdown. The unexpected compressor shutdown initiated the automated safety instrumented system (SIS). The SIS functioned as designed and routed process gas to the Olefin flares (EPN 10 and 11) for safe thermal destruction. The cause of the steam instability and subsequent compressor shutdown is currently being investigated by the site.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
Vent gas was routed to the Olefin's Wet Flare, EPN 10 and Dry Flare, EPN 11, for safe thermal destruction. Flaring was minimized to the extent practicable by reducing facility operating rates which minimizes the amount of material routed to the flare.
Basis Used to Determine Quantities and Any Additional Information Necessary to Evaluate the Event:
Emission calculations are based on the flare flow meters, flare gas chromatographs, laboratory analysis and engineering estimations and calculations. Offsite and fence line air monitoring was conducted during the event by the Refinery Terminal Fire Company (RTFC) and no emissions were detected. Affirmative defense for the event is being claimed pending the ongoing investigation.
