Cause of Emission Event or Excess Opacity Event, or Reason for Scheduled Activity:
Goldsmith Gas Plant started up operations on 7/10/2025 from a planned maintenance outage on the Sulfur Recovery Unit (SRU). Pursuant to 30 TAC 101.211(a), DCP submitted this initial notification as soon as practical prior to the scheduled start up activity.
Actions Taken, or Being Taken, to Minimize Emissions And/or Correct the Situation:
The Plant's Operators followed the start up procedures and minimized the duration and any emissions for this event. The site's flares were maintained in constant operation during this flare event, flare pilots were monitored, and no bypassing of the control device occurred.
