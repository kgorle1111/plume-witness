## Exact rules

- **Sites:** Beaumont-Downtown (48_245_0009), Nederland 17th Street (48_245_1035). Coordinates from TCEQ TAMIS.
- **Monitors per event:** the AutoGC sites within 15 km of the facility (a farther site's
  corridor can never reach it). A monitor without data or baseline for the day is dropped and recorded.
- **Detection (unchanged pipeline):** value >= max(10 x 7-day median, 10 ppb), earliest hour of the event's start day.
- **Corridor (unchanged pipeline):** +/-22.5 deg of upwind, <= 15.0 km, from the wind at the triggering monitor in the
  detection hour and the hour before (for a spike in hour 0, the previous day's 23:00; 0 spikes
  were in hour 0, and 0 of them had no cached previous day, so they read hour 0
  alone); a monitor with no wind uses the nearest site that has it, only within 10 km (recorded per
  event; farther, the spike has no wind and is not traced).
  The TCEQ met form returned no hourly wind for none; each uses its
  own AutoGC-form resultant wind (61104/61103) instead, and borrows only on a day that has neither. Wind source of
  the 2 spikes: own_met 2.
  Facilities come from EPA ECHO major sources within 10 mi of the triggering monitor.
- **Match (unchanged pipeline):** record_check picks this incident: in corridor, report [start, end] overlaps -6h..+2h of detection (it may have started on an earlier day). STEERS local time is converted with America/Chicago DST.
- **Day's reports:** every STEERS row that started that day, taken from the week search (same query, wider window),
  plus every row that started earlier and was still open (its Ended column) within 6 h of the day's first hour.
  Absence also needs the 30 days before covered by complete week searches, so spikes in early
  January 2024 always abstain. A report that started more than 30 days earlier is still matched
  when it is in the saved searches, but its absence is not guaranteed.
- **Calm wind:** when the wind at the detection hour or the hour before is under 3 mph, no
  corridor is drawn and the check abstains (*search incomplete*, "wind too light to trace").

## What this does NOT measure

- **False-alarm rate.** (c) counts spikes with no matching report, but nobody knows the true cause of those
  readings, so they cannot be scored as right or wrong; this is not a precision number.
- **Reports below detection.** A release that never reaches a monitor at
  10x baseline and 10 ppb counts as "not detected", whether or not
  any monitor could have seen it. 81 of the 81 candidates stop here, so this backtest mostly measures
  whether that trigger fires, and says little about the tracing on the events it never saw.
- **Next-day spikes.** Detection looks only at the event's start day (CST hours); a late-night release that
  reached a monitor after midnight counts as missed.
- **Causation.** A match means the timing and geometry are consistent with the report, not that this release
  caused the reading.
- **Facilities outside the candidate set** (0 unlocated, 1 beyond
  15 km) are not in any denominator.
- AutoGC data are labeled provisional by TCEQ and may change; a rerun on refetched data can differ.
